How we get the Elo

Each entry plays 1,200 games against Stockfish at three strengths, from 200 fixed openings with both colors. A game stops when one side is clearly winning.

BayesElo turns the results into ratings, with Stockfish 1500 at depth 1 pinned to 1500. So it is not a human rating.

The baseline scores 1303 ±16 over 1,200 games without legal masking; 691 games ended in a forfeit for an invalid prediction. Its previous masked score was 1517. Masked games are excluded from this rating pool. The v1 reevaluation is pending.

The rules, short version

File
One ONNX file, at most 10 MB, with the vocabulary in its metadata. No extra downloads.
Tokens
One SAN move per token. Bring your own list, or use ours (14,750 tokens). The highest-scoring token is your move. Illegal moves and non-move tokens lose immediately. No masking or retries; check and mate suffixes remain optional.
Input
Move history as your token ids, starting with <bos>
Output
One logit per token in your list
Context
64 to 4,096 tokens
Not allowed
Search, engines, hand-written heuristics
Training
Anything goes
